# Behaviour configuration and species policy

No new inheritable loci were added. Speed, life stage, sprint capacity/cost/recovery, appetite, perception, camouflage, alertness and existing mating traits continue to vary by individual. Relationship limits, route-cache bounds and goal arbitration are engine policy rather than additional biology genes.

| Controls                                                                         | Current meaning                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `creatureHerdingEnabled`                                                         | Enable the local social/threat engine. Disabled clears active herd steering/alarm information.                                                                                                |
| `creatureHerdingSimpleMode`, `creatureHerdingUseWorker`                          | Retained preset aliases; neither changes the authoritative engine or tick scheduling.                                                                                                         |
| `creatureSimpleHerdingCohesionRadius`                                            | Local neighbour awareness; geographical acquisition uses 1.5 × this distance and retention 1.8 × acquisition. It no longer means continuous attraction to a centre.                           |
| `creatureSimpleHerdingSeparationRadius/Strength`                                 | Tapered personal-space correction, capped below goal authority.                                                                                                                               |
| `creatureSimpleHerdingThreatRange`                                               | Upper bound for direct threat queries; contextual visibility and individual senses can shorten it.                                                                                            |
| `creatureThreatPropagationRadius/Hops`, `creatureThreatDecaySeconds`             | Local warning reach, depth and original warning lifetime. No multi-hop same-tick cascade or circular refresh.                                                                                 |
| `creatureHerdMovementContagionRadius/Delay/Chance`                               | Local observation and independent opportunities to respond to actual movement. Delay is the interval between attempts, not a per-render-frame probability.                                    |
| `creatureHerdingRegroupEnabled/Range`                                            | Occasional rejoin goals when isolated, with local same-species companions rather than a global centroid.                                                                                      |
| `creaturePackEnabled/Spacing`                                                    | Local pack membership and personal spacing. Acquisition is four spacing radii, retention wider; maximum eight members. Herbivore groups cap at 24.                                            |
| `creaturePredatorPatrolRadius`                                                   | Search sampling radius around the current position, not permanent home tether. Out-of-bounds candidates are rejected, not clamped onto map edges.                                             |

| `creaturePredatorPatrolMemoryDecay/Max/PreySightingWeight`                       | Bounded decaying actual sightings, revisited probabilistically; separate recent-search history caps at 16 sites.                                                                              |
| `creaturePredatorPatrolPauseDuration`                                            | Stop to scan after arriving; route commitments otherwise account for distance and movement speed.                                                                                             |
| `creaturePredatorRestEnabled/RestThreshold/HuntThreshold`                        | Appetite can trigger readiness earlier than the old minimum hunt threshold. Rest enters above the greater of the configured satiation threshold or appetite + 0.08, and leaves at readiness.  |
| `creatureWaterMemoryInHerdEnabled`                                               | Now defaults true. A group does not remove an individual's ability to remember observed water.                                                                                                |
| `creatureWaterRendezvousEnabled/MaxDistance/CommitSeconds`                       | Share known water locally and commit briefly; water still has to be observed or remembered.                                                                                                   |

Old alignment, cohesion-force, species-anchor, target-blend, graze-duty-cycle and short patrol-retarget controls are retained for imported presets but explicitly labelled **Legacy** and advanced in the editor. `creaturePredatorPatrolSpeed` was already unused and remains labelled unused; current motion uses the shared intent-speed settings. Removed implementation: global anchor/complex force engine and unused boids worker. There is one authoritative herd engine.

## Feeding change and rationale

The original design food web and audited runtime differed before this work. The runtime still allows triangles/octagons to consume meat, grass and berries; squares/circles retain their existing preferences and target-species weights are unchanged. The change concerns **when fallback plants are considered**, not a silent removal of diet identity or a forced match to old canon.

A meat-first predator normally perceives/selects meat as food and searches/hunts at its inherited appetite threshold. Below half that threshold it can select its existing plant fallbacks; a currently eating animal can recover another 0.1 energy ratio before leaving emergency feeding. For appetite 0.8, that means entering emergency fallback below 0.4 and completing recovery near 0.5. Existing trait-driven digestive efficiencies still determine energy gained. No energy is granted for searching or attacking.

Baseline predators often ate ubiquitous plants before the old 50% hunt threshold, while slow stalking persisted as prey visibly escaped. Controlled tests and full-world comparisons validate these distinct causal bottlenecks. The new policy lets meat support satiation/reproduction while plants can prevent immediate starvation. Predator reproduction is consequently harder when hunting fails; this is an observable survival consequence, not masked by hidden population targets. The evidence report records outcomes and extinctions.

Review correction: `creatureHerdingRegroupMinLocalHerdSize` is active and counts the creature itself. Disabling herding clears contagion/local membership as well as steering and alarms; disabling packs drops active membership, search and spacing state. Re-enabling creates fresh local packs. Land navigation and pack candidate selection use the world's configured water terrain, including non-default values; route caches include that type in their invalidation key.
