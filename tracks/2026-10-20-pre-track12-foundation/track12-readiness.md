# Track 12 integration readiness

The foundation provides persistent individual destinations, locally maintained group IDs, observed threat positions with finite expiry, current physical velocity/heading, land routes, bounded search memory and visible chase outcomes. These remain creature/world-owned, seeded and synchronous. No wounds, healing, hit rolls or multi-hit attacks were implemented.

| Track 12 concern                | Integration point and ownership                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Persistent combat engagement    | Extend `creature.chase` with engagement rather than clearing its target on the first close approach. Current `caught` finalizes the interim one-catch outcome; replace that transition in the combat track. |
| Multi-hit attacks and cooldowns | `combat.js` owns attack effects; creature-owned combat counters should count simulation ticks. Resolve an attack attempt separately from chase completion.                                                  |
| Hit probability                 | Passed seeded RNG, existing senses/alertness and explicit attack eligibility. Do not add random draws in rendering, inspector or telemetry.                                                                 |
| Evasion                         | Use actual relative velocity, range and heading from movement, with individual traits and stamina. Neighbour alignment no longer determines escape direction.                                               |
| Wounds affecting movement       | Apply an explicit injury scale at effective speed/turn calculation before velocity integration. Preserve inherited traits/genome; do not permanently overwrite phenotype values.                            |
| Disengagement                   | Existing chase loss, last observed position, stamina exhaustion, rest and finite prey alarm expiry supply separate pursuit and fear lifecycles. A fight can end without death.                              |
| Recovery and healing            | Costs/LifeHistory/regen stages can update creature-owned injury state after action resolution; survival intent and ordinary rest already have clear authority.                                              |
| Death and cleanup               | Existing death accounting/carcasses and local-group reconstruction remove dead members without entity references; test simultaneous attackers and leader death in the combat track.                         |

Current catch/combat runs at the start of a tick against the last completed positions, before new decisions/movement. This change preserves that documented discrete convention and refreshes the spatial index at the start and again after movement before reproduction. For multi-hit combat, explicitly decide whether attacks use pre- or post-movement positions; post-movement Act resolution is the natural location. Move attack resolution once, preserve stable attacker iteration, and rebuild/query the appropriate spatial snapshot. This is combat timing work for Track 12, not a missing movement system.

Remaining concerns are ecological duration and combat-specific design: a small founding predator population can fail to reproduce before age deaths, and catch frequency varies strongly by seed/regions. The interim catch is lethal; adding escape/evasion/wounds will change this pressure. Current ten-minute evidence does not establish multi-hour demographic equilibrium. Avoid compensating spawn/kill scripts. Rerun the recorded scenarios and long-seed suite after changing attack outcomes.
