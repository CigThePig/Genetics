# Spatial population ecology

Investigate movement relative to reproduction and lifetime; support geographic histories and permeable local populations without scripted divergence. Preserve survival authority, local social decisions, deterministic replay and browser practicality. Baseline is main 5740523 (PR 140).

Acceptance: measured paired movement/reproductive/genetic comparisons, neutral inheritance and bounded history, warranted behavioral changes with ablations, multi-seed multigeneration trials, regression and browser checks, documented limits and a PR against main. No predetermined divergence thresholds or population floors.
