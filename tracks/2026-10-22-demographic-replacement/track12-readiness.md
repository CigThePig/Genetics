# Track 12 integration readiness

The reproduction/life-history foundation supports controlled Combat + Wounds + Healing development. This is not a claim of ecological equilibrium or permanent predator persistence. Final hosted validation and its limitations are recorded in evidence.md and the source-pinned manifest.

| Question | Evidence / contract |
| --- | --- |
| Can favorable predators replace themselves? | Both predator species produce mature descendants and descendants with mature offspring; controlled two-founder runs can still fail naturally. |
| Are small failures understandable? | Same-sex, separation, depleted reserves and small-cohort chance remain effective. The diagnostics do not automatically certify every wild extinction as natural. |
| Are rules shared across all four species? | Same genetic maturity, longevity coupling, parental recovery and newborn initialization; both birth paths are covered. |
| Can offspring mature and reproduce? | Multigeneration assays, all-species maturity/parentage tests and observed wild mature descendants. |
| Do pregnancy/birth transitions work? | Conception, birth, miscarriage, active pregnancy and maternal-loss accounting is conserved. |
| Can future failures be diagnosed? | Pair-attempt/failure counters, exposure funnel, cohorts, pedigree output, explicit censoring and death causes. |
| Are real opportunities available for combat studies? | Favorable full-movement approach cases and default seeded births/hunts; include adverse and small-cohort controls. |
| Is mortality accounting suitable? | Explicit lethal predation precedes fallback age/injury/thirst/starvation; simultaneous conditions remain defined. |
| Where do wounds integrate? | Reproductive penalties belong in the shared eligibility predicate; mobility penalties belong in movement/traits. Do not fake either with a juvenile/reproductive cooldown. |
| What risks remain? | Sparse partners, unmatched reserve windows, sex loss, source-defined interaction radius, growing population cost, finite study horizons and physical-phone profiling. |

Preserve Sense → Decide → Act → Costs → LifeHistory → Regen → Metrics. Reproduction owns conception, gestation and parental recovery. Movement owns positions. Death owns terminal attribution/removal. A future nonlethal wound must not be labelled a lethal predation event, silently clear pregnancy, alter inherited maturity or overload cooldown ownership.

Compare successful hunting and actual food energy alongside attempts, failures, conceptions, births, juvenile survival, mature descendants and deaths with exposure/censoring. More kills, more births or survivors at a chosen endpoint alone cannot validate combat.

No population-dependent rescue, migration quotas, remote mate knowledge, lifespan immunity or fertility bonuses are introduced. Future combat tests need favorable opportunity controls and default/unfavorable ecology, not a survival target.
