## Accepted implementation

A universal **haploid quantitative-locus recombination** model copies one parental normalized value with probability 1/2 at each locus. Loci are unlinked. It is not a diploid/dominance model, and not a claim of biological genetic realism. It retains the flat genome, genotype-to-trait mapping, conception-time paternal copies, both birth paths, circular hue wrapping, mutation and inheritance noise. Existing organisms never change their inherited traits during life. Local ecological decisions and major tick order are untouched.

`creatureGenomeInheritanceMode` selects `segregating` (new-world default) or `blended` (legacy reproduction). The old mix chance applies only to blended inheritance. Standalone inheritance calls with no new setting retain the old behavior. Existing saved settings/presets merge with defaults; old presets lacking the key therefore use the new default when rebuilt. Set `blended` explicitly to reproduce old dynamics. The app saves settings, not whole live populations; no diploid migration or per-creature payload increase is needed. The neutral diagnostic diploid markers remain separate and have no phenotype effects.

## Rejected approaches

- Increasing mutation to counter blending: current mutation already maintains variance; it would replace rather than preserve selected alternatives and change a second cause.
- A diploid/dominance/linkage architecture: not necessary to test the diagnosed contraction; doubles genotype state and expands migration/pregnancy obligations without evidence of a compensating benefit here.
- More genes, species, learning/planning frameworks or Track 12 combat: no demonstrated requirement for this correction.
- Habitat-specific adaptive assignment or automatic extinction prevention: invalidates the governing objective.
- Declaring stronger variance or larger census an ecological success: neither demonstrates adaptation.
- Initially weak imposed selection (energy budget 1, deadline 20 seconds) produced only small changes; it is retained as a negative/weak-pressure result. The stronger assay uses explicitly specified .03 energy and 3.5 seconds to establish response capability, not to represent normal wild conditions.

## Remaining causal work

Field genotype associations are not randomized causal estimates. Geography, relatedness, sexual selection, age structure, mutation and chance can all shift distributions. A common-garden/reciprocal-transfer program with replicated habitat selection ablations is still needed before claiming local adaptation. Similarly, observer screenshots establish readable information and actual behavior, not that a viewer now finds the simulation compelling. The user remains the judge of that experience.
