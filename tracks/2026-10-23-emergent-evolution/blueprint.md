# Investigation design

Start with existing source and evidence. Isolate inheritance contraction from mutation, selection and drift. Compare simple per-locus parental segregation with legacy blending before choosing a production default. Keep the flat quantitative genome and existing trait/cost mappings: no diploid realism without evidence, hidden adaptive assignment or population correction.

Offline observers retain copied genomes, parent identities, maturation/death and sampled activity; they never intervene. Production history must remain fixed in size, world-owned, passive and independent of RNG. Compare live distributions to founders without labeling change as adaptation. Controlled selection assays must expose their imposed environments and distinguish mechanism tests from wild ecology.

Wild treatment seeds are fixed before inspecting outcomes: 1, 42, 1337, 2, 7, 19; 72,000 ticks each, with longer canonical runs as feasible. Baseline is an immutable worktree. Ecological RNG trajectories may diverge following different inheritance draws. Compare replicated outcomes rather than treating later events as matched counterfactuals.
